About the museum

A small museum with a long story

The Museum of Ancient Keys began with one private collection of forty keys. Today it fills twelve halls with locks, keys and tools from Egypt, Rome, Central Asia and medieval Europe.

We believe every key tells a story about the people who used it: what they owned, what they feared and what they wanted to protect.

From wood to steel

A Short History of the Key

How a simple wooden peg became a clever mechanism.

Ancient Egypt

4,000 years ago

The first pin locks appear. Keys are large and made of wood.

Ancient Rome

1st – 4th centuries

Romans make keys of bronze and iron. They become small and strong.

The Middle Ages

10th – 15th centuries

Blacksmiths build locks with tricky wards inside. Keys become works of art.

The Modern Era

18th – 19th centuries

The cylinder lock is invented. Keys look almost like the ones we use today.

Good to know

Questions

Can I take photos inside?

Yes, photos without flash are allowed in every hall except the treasury.

How long does a visit take?

Most visitors spend about one and a half hours in the museum.

Is the museum accessible?

All halls are on one floor and have wide doors for wheelchairs.

Can I donate an old key?

Yes. Send an email to info@museumofkeys.uz and our curators will reply.
